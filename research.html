Research centers

AdRIC, the College’s research institute, was founded in 1994 to set research priorities and fund projects, and to seek partnerships with industry, government and academia. Director: Dr. Joel Ilao.

Lab

Bioinformatics Lab

Applies computer science and engineering to biological questions: algorithms and software for large sequencing datasets, with work on antimicrobial resistance, HIV and cancer, and hardware designs that speed up bioinformatics.

Lead Anish MS Shrestha

CAR

Center for Automation Research

Develops control systems, machines and information technologies that make processes, goods and services more efficient, along with tools for gathering and interpreting data.

Lead Clement Y. Ong

COMET

Center for Complexity and Emerging Technologies

A multidisciplinary lab studying complex adaptive systems and how people interact with computing, and building civic tech tools from the research.

Lead Briane Paul V. Samson

CITe4D

Center for ICT for Development

Research and innovation on using digital technologies to address human development and poverty, with public and private partners.

Lead Oliver Malabanan

CeLT

Center for Language Technologies

Applies computational methods to human language, with a focus on natural language processing for Philippine languages, hate speech and disinformation.

Lead Edward P. Tighe

CeHCI

Center for Human-Computer Innovations

Builds human-centered systems that read emotion from facial expressions, voice and body movement, so sensor-rich spaces can respond with empathy.

CNIS

Center for Networking and Information Security

Studies wireless and software-defined networks, IoT and cloud systems, and both attacking and defending computer systems.

Lead Marnel S. Peradilla

CIVI

Computational Imaging and Visual Innovations

Applies deep learning to image data for practical problems: dataset creation, model design and optimization, and applications.

Lead Arren Matthew Antioquia

GAME Lab

Graphics, Animation, Multimedia and Entertainment Laboratory

Creative computing in game development, computer graphics, AR/VR and digital twin simulations.

Lead Neil Patrick A. Del Gallego

TE3D House

Technology, Education, Entertainment, Empathy, Design House

Based at the Laguna campus. Builds practical, deployable software and apps for education, empathy and entertainment, guided by people-first design.

Lead Dr. Judith J. Azcarraga
