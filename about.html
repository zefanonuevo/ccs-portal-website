About CCS.

The College of Computer Studies at De La Salle University, Manila: teaching, research, product development and community service.

Vision and mission

What the College stands for.

Vision

Faith-driven scholars, serving the nation.

A community of faith-driven scholars dedicated to national development and social welfare. CCS aims to be Southeast Asia’s leading institution for computer science and ICT, and the Philippines’ standard for quality education and research.

Mission

Five commitments

  • Grow a culture of whole-person development.
  • Develop adaptable leaders in technical, professional and entrepreneurial fields.
  • Drive innovation through advanced research and modern learning facilities.
  • Create immersion, consulting and technology commercialization opportunities with industry, government and academic partners.
  • Inspire the community to work for the common good, especially for the poor and marginalized.

The College’s five strategic goals sit on Jubilee Progress.

Recognition

Accreditation and awards the College holds.

First PAASCU-accredited CS program

The BS Computer Science program was the first computer science program in the Philippines accredited by PAASCU.

PAASCU Level III, since 2005

The College earned Level III accreditation in 2005 and still holds it.

Center of Excellence, 2007

CHED recognized CCS as a Center of Excellence in IT Education in the National Capital Region.
