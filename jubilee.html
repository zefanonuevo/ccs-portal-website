Five strategic goals.

What the College is working toward. Highlights below show progress against them.

Goal 1

Keep programs current

Degree programs stay up to date as technology changes.

Goal 2

Competent, ethical graduates

Graduates are professionally skilled and act responsibly and ethically in computing.

Goal 3

Research that lasts

Research output and quality rise to a level the College can sustain.

Goal 4

Faculty and staff growth

Skills and attitudes grow in teaching, professional practice, organizational capacity, cultural awareness, and health and wellness.

Goal 5

Lasting partnerships

Long-term partnerships with communities and organizations improve quality of life and benefit society.
